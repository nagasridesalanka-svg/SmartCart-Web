Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about orders, payments, warranties, and returns.

What is your standard delivery timeframe and shipping cost?
We offer standard tracked courier delivery across all regions within 2 to 4 business days for a flat rate of $9.99. Orders exceeding $150 qualify for 100% FREE standard shipping automatically applied at checkout.
How do I track the courier transit status of my order?
As soon as our fulfillment warehouse processes your package, you can monitor milestone progression under the Order History tab. You can also view live delivery addresses and item snapshots.
What is SmartCart's return and refund policy?
We offer a comprehensive 30-day return policy. Items must be returned in their original packaging and undamaged condition. Once received and inspected at our return center, your full refund is processed within 3 to 5 business days.
Which payment methods are accepted during checkout?
SmartCart accepts all major Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), UPI and Instant Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD). In this preview environment, payments are simulated with instant confirmation.
What should I do if an item arrives damaged or defective?
Please open a ticket in the 'Create Ticket' tab with the issue type 'Damaged Product'. These tickets are automatically prioritized with High urgency, and our dedicated team will arrange a free immediate replacement or full refund.
Can I modify my shipping address or cancel an order after placing it?
Orders can be updated or cancelled as long as they remain in the 'Placed' status before courier hand-off. Please submit a support ticket immediately with your Order ID, or update your default address in your Account Profile.
Do electronic items and peripherals include warranty coverage?
Yes, every electronic device, audio equipment, and mechanical peripheral sold on SmartCart includes a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering internal hardware malfunctions, charging controller defects, and component failures.
How are refunds credited back to my payment account?
Approved refunds are credited automatically back to the original method of payment (Card or UPI). For Cash on Delivery orders, our support representative will contact you to securely arrange an instant bank transfer.
